## 1. Product Direction

The R0.7 goal has shifted from "explain the product with text" to "the website design itself is the strongest marketing proof."

Design target:

- Premium, technology-forward, Apple-like restraint.
- Fewer explanatory paragraphs.
- More high-fidelity product/interface preview.
- Customer should understand the value by seeing the quality of the site.
- The homepage should not contain every subpage; it should route to the right page.

Core customer promise:

> Premium websites for service businesses that cannot afford missed leads.

## 8. Review Standard

R0.7 should not pass only because pages compile.

It should pass when:

- The site looks premium enough to prove the service quality.
- A customer can understand what they get without long explanation.
- Design quality itself is a sales argument.
- Forms still work through the dynamic engine.
- No R0.8 heavy admin/CRM scope is introduced accidentally.
