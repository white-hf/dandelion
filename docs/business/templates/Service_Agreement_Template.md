## 3. Scope

Starter includes:

- one mobile-friendly business website;
- standard template layout;
- business information sections;
- service sections;
- contact form;
- phone CTA;
- basic privacy notice;
- hosting and SSL;
- basic maintenance.

Growth and Managed may include additional pages, lead alerts, AI lead summaries, or monthly updates if explicitly listed.

## 4. Revisions

Starter includes:

- one revision round before launch;
- corrections to business name, phone, email, hours, service area;
- up to 5 service text changes;
- up to 10 photo replacements if the client provides authorized photos.

Out of scope unless separately quoted:

- custom design from scratch;
- unlimited revisions;
- logo design;
- SEO campaign;
- paid ads management;
- booking/payment/e-commerce systems;
- custom software integrations;
- copywriting beyond included package scope.

## 5. Client Responsibilities

Client must:

- provide accurate business information;
- confirm phone, email, service area, hours, and services;
- provide or authorize any logo, photo, review, testimonial, or third-party content used on the site;
- provide domain/DNS access if using an existing domain;
- review the site before launch;
- pay setup and monthly fees on time.

## 6. Content Authorization

Client confirms they own or have permission to use all content they provide.

Client authorizes Dandelion to use approved content for the website while the service is active.

Third-party platform content, including Google/Facebook/Yelp reviews or photos, may be subject to platform rules. Dandelion may remove disputed or non-compliant content.

## 7. Preview Sites

If Dandelion created an unofficial preview before this agreement:

- the preview was not an official client website;
- the preview may have used publicly available business facts and AI-generated copy;
- final launch requires client approval and content authorization.

## 8. Payment Terms

- Setup fee is due before activation work begins.
- Monthly fee is billed in advance.
- Setup fee is non-refundable after activation work begins.
- Third-party fees may be billed separately if not included.
- Failed payments may result in service pause after notice.

Suggested failed payment process:

- Day 0: failed payment notice.
- Day 7: second notice.
- Day 14: site may be paused.
- Day 30: account may be cancelled.

## 9. Hosting And Maintenance

Monthly fee covers:

- website hosting;
- SSL;
- uptime monitoring;
- basic form smoke checks;
- minor updates included in the selected plan.

Maintenance does not include new features, redesigns, large content rewrites, custom integrations, or SEO campaigns unless separately agreed.

## 10. No Guarantees

Dandelion does not guarantee:

- Google ranking;
- number of leads;
- revenue;
- review growth;
- ad performance;
- uninterrupted third-party platform availability.

## 11. Privacy And Data

Dandelion will collect only the data needed to operate the website and contact forms.

Client is responsible for reviewing whether the site meets their industry-specific legal, privacy, or regulatory requirements.

For regulated industries, additional review may be required before launch.

## 12. Cancellation

Client may cancel monthly service with `[30]` days notice.

Upon cancellation:

- Dandelion may disable hosting after the paid period ends;
- client may request export of website content and lead data;
- domain ownership depends on who purchased/owns the domain;
- unpaid balances remain due.

## 13. Intellectual Property

Client owns client-provided content.

Dandelion owns reusable templates, software modules, internal tools, prompts, and automation systems.

Client receives a license to use the website while the service is active and paid.

If client wants full static export or source transfer, it must be separately agreed.

## 14. Limitation Of Liability

Dandelion is not liable for indirect, incidental, special, consequential, or lost-profit damages.

Total liability is limited to fees paid by client in the previous `[3]` months, unless prohibited by law.
